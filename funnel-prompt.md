# Portafor — Selling Funnel Webpage Prompt

## Overview

Build a single-page marketing funnel for Portafor. The page converts visitors into signups. No navigation, no footer links — one focused path to the CTA.

## Page Structure (top to bottom)

### 1. Hero Section
- **Headline:** "Stop chasing leads. Let permits come to you."
- **Subheadline:** "Portafor monitors real-time building permits in Austin and Orlando. When a new roofing, HVAC, or solar permit drops in your territory — you get an instant email alert."
- **Primary CTA:** "Start free for 30 days" → `/sign-up`
- **Trust line:** "No credit card required. Cancel anytime."

### 2. How It Works (3 columns)
1. **Draw your territory** — "Drop a pin or draw a polygon. We watch that area 24/7."
2. **We monitor permits** — "Every new building permit filed with the city is checked against your zone."
3. **Get instant alerts** — "Receive an email the moment a matching permit appears."

### 3. Pain Point Section
**Header:** "You're losing leads to contractors who check permit sites first."
- "City websites update slowly and are hard to search"
- "By the time you check, another contractor already called"
- "You can't monitor 10 cities manually"
- **Resolution:** "Portafor does the checking for you — every hour, automatically."

### 4. Stats Bar
- "2,000+ permits monitored"
- "Austin & Orlando active"
- "Alerts sent within 1 hour of filing"

### 5. Features (2x2 grid)
- **Real-time monitoring** — "We poll city data feeds hourly."
- **Territory mapping** — "Draw your exact service area on an interactive map."
- **Email alerts** — "Permit details delivered to your inbox."
- **Filing search** — "Search any address to find nearby permits."

### 6. Pricing
- **Free for 30 days** — full access
- **Then $49/month** — cancel anytime
- CTA: "Start your trial" → `/sign-up`

### 7. Final CTA
Dark teal strip:
- "Every permit is a homeowner who needs your services."
- "Start finding them today."
- CTA: "Get started free" → `/sign-up`

## Design
- No stock photos, text-driven
- Mobile-first
- Warm, contractor-friendly tone
- CTA: `bg-teal-700 hover:bg-teal-800 text-white rounded-xl`
- Section spacing: `py-16 lg:py-24`
